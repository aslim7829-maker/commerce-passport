● Product registration lookup

Know what you're buying.

Check whether a product is registered in Commerce Passport. Enter the product ID and the serial number printed on the product or its registration label.

This is the product's UUID, not the serial number. The product owner can find the UUID in the product record in Supabase.

A successful result confirms that the supplied product ID and serial number match a registered record. It is not, by itself, proof that a physical item has not been copied or tampered with.
